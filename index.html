Final itinerary

Day by day

Rail/bus Car Slow day
2 NovArrive TokyoShinjuku

Arrive NRT around 15:35, transfer to Shinjuku, eat near the hotel and sleep; no scheduled sightseeing.

3 NovA relaxed Tokyo dayNo car

Choose one compact area rather than crossing the city repeatedly: Yanesen, Meiji Jingu–Omotesando, or a garden-and-food day.

4 NovTokyo → Matsumoto~2h 40m rail

Take the Azusa from Shinjuku, leave luggage near Matsumoto Station, then visit Matsumoto Castle, Nakamachi and Nawate Street.

5 NovMatsumoto → Kamikochi → Hirayu~55 km driving
~1h 20m driving~25m each way shuttleLeave Kamikochi 14:00–14:30

Collect the car at 08:00, drive to Akandana/Hirayu parking, shuttle into Kamikochi and take a gentle Taisho Pond–Kappa Bridge walk; return to Hirayu for check-in and an onsen evening.

6 NovShinhotaka + Hirayu → Takayama~70 km
~1h 45m drivingWeather-dependent ropewayOnsen stop

Ride Shinhotaka Ropeway early, return through Hirayu for lunch and Hirayu-no-Mori’s day-use baths, then reach Takayama by approximately 16:00.

7 NovShirakawa-go day trip~100 km
~1h 50m total drivingEarly start

Reach Ogimachi before peak coach traffic, see the Shiroyama viewpoint and village lanes, then return to Takayama for a relaxed evening.

8 NovTakayama + Hida-Furukawa~35 km
~50m total drivingSlow Hida day

Visit Miyagawa Market, Takayama Jinya and Sanmachi in the morning, then spend a quiet afternoon beside Hida-Furukawa’s canals and white storehouses.

9 NovTakayama → Gero → Kiso-Fukushima~145 km
~3h driving2–3h Gero stop

Follow the Hida River south, stop in Gero for the riverside, lunch and an optional day bath, then cross to Kiso-Fukushima before dark.

10 NovAtera + Tsumago + Magome loop~125–140 km
~3h drivingFull countryside dayNo long hike

Start with Nezame-no-Toko and Atera Valley, continue to Tsumago and Magome for short village walks, then return to the same Kiso-Fukushima hotel; omit Nezame first if the day runs late.

11 NovKiso-Fukushima → Narai → Matsumoto~75 km
~1h 40m driving1½–2h Narai stop

Explore Kiso-Fukushima briefly, drive north to Narai’s long preserved street and cafés, then sleep beside Matsumoto Station for an easy return.

12 NovReturn car → Kofu1 night

Refuel and return the car to the exact Toyota Matsumoto Station branch, then take the train to Kofu; keep the afternoon light rather than squeezing in Shosenkyo.

13 NovShosenkyo → Kawaguchiko1 Fuji night

Take an early Kofu bus to Shosenkyo, walk the upper gorge and optionally ride the ropeway, then continue to Kawaguchiko for check-in and the Momiji Corridor illumination.

14 NovFuji morning → Tokyo by sunsetIkebukuro/Otsuka
Sunrise first~13:00 leave Kawaguchiko~15:00–16:00 Tokyo

Use the morning for the north shore, Momiji Corridor or Oishi Park according to visibility; depart around 13:00—not late afternoon—to reach Tokyo around sunset.

15 NovTokyo flex dayNo car

Use this for neighbourhoods, shopping, food or the best live foliage report; avoid committing it months ahead.

16 NovKamakura day tripTokyo

Kita-Kamakura temples, central Kamakura and Hase provide the calm-town and coastal element, even though foliage will probably be early.

17 NovDepart from NaritaFlight day

For a 14:20 departure, leave Ikebukuro/Otsuka around 09:00–09:30 and allow generous transfer and airport time.

Driving caution

Early-November snow or overnight ice is possible around Hirayu and Shinhotaka; studless tyres are strongly preferred, and Shinhotaka should be skipped if road or wind conditions deteriorate.
