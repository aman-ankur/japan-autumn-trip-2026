Why this window Mid-November is the strongest overlap for Kiso Valley, Takayama, Shirakawa-go and the Fuji foothills. A 27–28 October start is possible, but it requires a different foliage strategy—not merely shifting these same dates earlier.

If we start 27–28 October instead
Gain

Higher mountains

Late October better supports Hakuba, Itoigawa valleys and a final-season Kamikochi visit.

Lose

Core-route colour

Kiso’s southern post towns and Fuji are likely too early; Takayama may only be beginning.

Verdict

Do not just shift dates

Use a northern alpine itinerary instead. For this exact Kiso–Takayama–Fuji route, keep mid-November.

Kamikochi’s official season ends on 15 November, but late-season conditions can be cold and the best autumn colour generally occurs earlier.

Keep, consider, skip

What the video route changed—and what it should not

Definitely add

Hida-Furukawa

The strongest upgrade: quiet canals and white storehouses, only a modest addition after Shirakawa-go.

Optional

Gero + Daio

Gero works as an en-route bath; Daio Wasabi Farm works only with an early start from Matsumoto.

Optional

Yamanakako

A fresh Fuji perspective by bus, avoiding a second car rental.

Leave out

Hakuba + Kamikochi

Better for late October. At your recommended dates they are colder, later-season and more walking-heavy.

Separate trip

Kurobe + Kanazawa

Both are excellent, but turn this compact loop into a much larger regional circuit.

Not worth the logistics

Nagoya + Fujinomiya

They supported the video creator’s one-way rentals, not your priorities or same-branch rule.
